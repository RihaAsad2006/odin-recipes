Classic Garlic Butter Chicken

Juicy chicken breast pieces cooked in a rich, flavorful garlic butter sauce with fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 300g chicken breast (boneless, cubed)
  • 2 tbsp butter & 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt, black pepper, and dried parsley

Steps

  1. Season Chicken:

    Toss chicken cubes with salt and black pepper.

  2. Sear Meat:

    Heat olive oil in a pan and cook chicken until golden brown and cooked through; remove from pan.

  3. Make Sauce:

    Melt butter in the same pan, add minced garlic, and sauté for 1 minute.

  4. Combine:

    Return chicken to the pan, pour in lemon juice, and stir well for 2 minutes.

  5. Garnish:

    Sprinkle with parsley and serve warm.
