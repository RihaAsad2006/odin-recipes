Lasagna Recipe

This classic homemade lasagna features rich layers of savory meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tender pasta baked to golden perfection. It is a comforting, crowd-pleasing dish that brings warm Italian flavors straight to your dinner table.

Ingredients

    Meat Sauce

  • Ground beef (or a mix of beef and Italian sausage)
  • Chopped onion and garlic
  • Canned crushed tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Olive oil, salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning (basil, oregano)

    • Cheese Filling

  • Ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg (helps bind the cheese mixture)
  • Fresh parsley (chopped)

    • Pasta

  • Lasagna noodles (boiled or oven-ready)

Steps

  1. Make the Sauce:

    Cook onions, garlic, and ground beef, then stir in tomato sauce and spices to simmer.

  2. Boil Pasta:

    Cook noodles in salted water until soft, then drain.

  3. Mix Cheese:

    Stir together ricotta, mozzarella, Parmesan, and one egg in a bowl.

  4. Layer:

    Spread sauce on a baking dish, then add layers of noodles, cheese mixture, and meat sauce.

  5. Bake:

    Top with extra mozzarella, cover with foil, and bake at 190°C for 35 minutes until bubbly.

  6. Rest:

    Cool for 10 minutes, slice, and serve.
